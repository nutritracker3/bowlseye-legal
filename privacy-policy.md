# bowlseye Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026
**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data bowlseye ("we," "us," "the App") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. It describes what the App's code does in this version.

**In short:** bowlseye keeps your food diary on your phone and, if you sign in, backs it up and syncs it to your account. It does not send your food to any AI service. We do not sell your data and there is no advertising.

## 1. Who we are

bowlseye is a nutrition and meal-logging app. The data controller for purposes of GDPR is **Vinay Goudar (India)**. Contact: **goudar.v@gmail.com**.

## 2. What data we collect

| Data | What specifically | Why | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account** | Name and email from Google Sign-In (and from Sign in with Apple on iPhone, where offered), and an account ID | Sign-in, and syncing your data across devices | Required to use the App |
| **About you** | Sex, age, height, country, and if you fill them in your city, occupation and Instagram handle, activity level, goal, how many meals you eat, how long you like to cook, dietary preferences, and the daily calorie and macro targets worked out from them | Working out your targets and your plan | Required to set up |

**Clipboard:** the App reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap Paste in the dish sheet, to bring an answer back. It does not read it in the background.

## 3. Special category data (GDPR) / sensitive personal information (CCPA)

Health and nutrition data is a **special category of personal data** under GDPR Article 9 and **sensitive personal information** under the CCPA/CPRA. We process it only:

- With your consent (by using the App's logging features), and
- As strictly necessary to provide the App's core functionality.

We do not use health data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers.

## 4. When a dish is not in the food list

bowlseye has a built-in food list and a central food data library built from dishes people have taught it.

- **Looking a dish up:** when a dish you type is not found, its **name** is sent to our server to check the central food data.
- **No AI in the App:** the App does not send your food to an AI service. When a dish is unknown, it copies a question (the dish name and what to answer) that **you** paste into an AI chat you choose. That chat is outside bowlseye, and its provider's own privacy policy applies to what you type there.
- **Adding to the central food data:** when you paste an answer back, the **dish name and its nutrition numbers** may be added to the central food data, so that other people searching for the same dish can find it. A private record of which account added a dish is kept on our server so a wrong entry can be traced and removed; it is never shown to other people. Numbers you **type yourself** stay on your phone and are never added.
- **Replies you choose to send us:** if a pasted reply does not work, the App offers "Send this reply to the developer". That opens your phone's share sheet with the dish name and the reply. **Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it**, and it goes where you choose. The App uploads nothing by itself. We use such replies only to improve how bowlseye reads replies.

## 5. Who we share data with (processors)

| Processor | What they receive | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database region: Mumbai, India) | Your account, profile, food diary, weigh-ins, supplements, activity, friends and competition data; the dish names looked up and added to central food data; the IP and security records above | Sign-in, cloud sync, central food data, friend features |
| **Google** | Your Google account identity at sign-in | Sign in with Google |
| **Apple** | Your Apple identity at sign-in, on iPhone where offered | Sign in with Apple |
| **PostHog** (United States) | Anonymous usage events and failure codes | Product analytics |
| **Sentry** | Technical crash reports without your name or email | Fixing bugs |

All transmission is over HTTPS. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Friends and competition members can see only what section 2 lists for them.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **On-device data:** kept until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- **Synced cloud data:** kept until you delete your account or data, or delete individual entries in the App.
- **Central food data:** a dish name with its nutrition numbers, not tied to you, so it stays after you delete your account.
- **Analytics events:** kept for up to **12 months**, then deleted or aggregated.
- **IP address counters:** deleted after about 3 days.
- **Crash reports and security records:** kept as long as needed to fix problems and keep the service safe.

## 7. Your rights

### If you're in the EU/UK/EEA (GDPR)

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it ("right to erasure"), restrict or object to processing, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

### If you're a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, delete it, correct inaccurate information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share it).

### Exercising your rights / requesting deletion

- **In the App:** Profile → Data & Privacy → **Delete all my data** permanently deletes the data on your device and your account with its synced records. **Clear meal history** deletes only your logged meals. **Export data** gives you a copy.
- **Analytics:** Profile → Data & Privacy → Export data has the "Anonymous usage analytics" switch.
- **By email:** contact **goudar.v@gmail.com**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (30 days under CCPA, one month under GDPR, extendable once if needed).

## 8. Cookies and tracking

bowlseye is a native mobile app, not a website, and does not use browser cookies. The analytics tool uses a locally generated anonymous identifier (not a cookie) to group events; it is not linked to your name, email or account.

## 9. Children's privacy

bowlseye is for adults, 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 10. Data security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Synced data is protected by row-level security so only your signed-in account can read or write your rows. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. International data transfers

Our database and sign-in infrastructure (Supabase) is hosted in Mumbai, India, and PostHog is hosted in the United States. If you use the App from another country, your data is transferred to and processed there. For EU/UK/EEA users, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another recognized transfer mechanism as GDPR requires.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date when this policy changes. Material changes will be communicated in the App.
