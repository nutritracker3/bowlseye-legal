# Delete your bowlseye account and data

You can delete your account and everything saved to it at any time, whether or not you still have the app.

## If you have the app

1. Open bowlseye and go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Data & Privacy** and tap **Delete all my data**.
3. Confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your meals, weigh-ins, profile, streaks and friends from our servers, and everything stored on your phone. It cannot be undone.

If you signed in but never finished setting up, open the app, choose **I already have an account**, sign in with the same Google account, and tap **Delete this account** on the "No account found" message.

## If you no longer have the app

Email **goudar.v@gmail.com** from the address of the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject **Delete my bowlseye account**. We will delete the account and its data and reply to confirm. We aim to do this within 30 days, and sooner where we can.

## What is deleted, and what is not

- **Deleted:** your account, profile, logged meals, weigh-ins, settings, streaks, friend connections and any friend invites you created.
- **Not linked to you:** dishes you taught the app may stay in the shared dish library as numbers only, with no account details. Anonymous usage analytics are not linked to your name, email or account.
- **Backups:** deleted records can remain in provider backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

More detail is in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).
