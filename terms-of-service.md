## 4. Numbers you paste or type

When a dish is not in the food list, the App can copy a question that you paste into an AI chat of your choice, and you paste the answer back. You can also type the numbers yourself. These numbers are estimates **you choose to enter**. bowlseye checks that they add up, but it cannot verify them: check them against the pack or menu when you can. The AI chat is a separate service that is not part of bowlseye, and its provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.

Dishes you teach the App by pasting an answer may be added to the App's central food data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Numbers you type yourself stay on your phone.
